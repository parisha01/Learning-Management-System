About Northlight

We think progress should be easy to see

Northlight LMS started as a frustration with spreadsheets. Training coordinators were tracking course completion across a dozen tabs, and nobody — not the learner, not the manager — could answer "how are we doing?" without a half-hour of scrolling. We build software that answers that question in one glance.

Our mission

Make every learning journey visible

Whether it's a new hire working through onboarding or a whole school district rolling out a safety module, Northlight gives everyone — learners, instructors and administrators — the same honest view of where things stand.

We keep the product deliberately calm: clear course cards, an honest progress ledger, and no dashboard chart-junk that looks impressive but tells you nothing.

Our story

How Northlight came together

2022

A side project for one training team

Northlight began as an internal tool for a 40-person customer support team tired of chasing quiz scores over email.

2023

First school pilot

A local secondary school piloted Northlight for a digital-literacy elective, and the drag-and-drop course builder was born from their feedback.

2024

Certificates and the progress ledger

Automatic certificates and the ledger-style dashboard shipped, turning raw completion data into something learners actually wanted to check.

2026

Northlight today

Now used by training teams and classrooms who want one clear home for courses, discussion and progress — with no spreadsheet required.
